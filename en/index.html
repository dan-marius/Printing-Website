The right material for the place

We choose the material by where the part will live: indoors, near a heat source, in a car or outside.

PLA≤ 55 °C

Indoors

Rigid, with a clean finish and many colours. Good for holders, organisers and covers that stay indoors, away from heat and sun.

PETG≤ 75 °C

Bathroom, kitchen, workshop

More resistant to moisture, impacts and moderate heat. Suits pipe covers, parts next to a radiator or bathtub, and functional parts.

ASA≤ 90 °C

Outdoors and cars

Handles sun (UV), rain, frost and the heat inside a car parked in summer. For outdoor parts and car interiors.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does a 3D printed part cost?

    It depends on the size and how much design work it needs. As a guide: small parts start at 30 RON, medium parts at 70 RON and large parts at 150 RON. 3D design costs 100 RON per hour. The calculator on this page gives you an instant estimate.

    How do I order a part?

    Send a photo of the part or of the place it has to fit, plus a few measurements, on WhatsApp or through the form. You get the price and timing before we start.

    Can you remake a broken part you can no longer buy?

    Yes. We remake it from the original part, even if it is broken, or from measurements. Sometimes we can make it stronger than the original.

    Do you come to measure at my home?

    Yes, in Baia Mare. A visit costs 50 RON (measuring, delivery or fitting) and is charged even if, once there, we find we can't help or we don't reach an agreement.

    Which materials do you use?

    PLA for indoor parts, PETG for places with moisture or moderate heat (bathroom, kitchen, next to a radiator) and ASA for outdoors and cars, where the part is exposed to sun and high temperatures.

    Do you deliver outside Baia Mare?

    Yes, by courier anywhere in Romania (25 RON). Simple parts can be made from photos and measurements alone, without a visit.

    I already have a 3D file (STL). Can you print it?

    Yes. Send the file by email or WhatsApp and we'll quote the printing, with no design cost.
